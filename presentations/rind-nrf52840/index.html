26-bit Poly1305 is the 32-bit path. This board's ratio is from an earlier image

The hardware numbers were not re-measured on this trim. They are prebuilt images of rind-hardware patches/poly1305-32. Re-measure on this commit before treating the ratios as current.
  • The PR's sentence for this board: ChaCha20-Poly1305 seal 16 KB is 0.698 of the HACL* image.
  • The same compare, which the PR points at, records that rind row as 895,189 → 624,562 cycles. Open 16 KB is 918,865 → 648,242 (0.705).
  • Rind long-input ChaCha median is 0.698 (range 0.691 to 0.705). Short-input median is 0.690 (0.658 to 0.699). Known-answer checks 21/21 on both images, 3 runs each.
  • Board: Seeed T1000-E, Cortex-M4F, 64 MHz, DWT_CYCCNT. Image sha256 matched rind-hardware 7a14b38 patches/poly1305-32/SHA256SUMS. The images were not rebuilt for #87.
