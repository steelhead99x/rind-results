Why the new addition is safe here

  • The routine needs the two points to be different. A Lean lemma, Rind.P256Booth.booth_ne, already proves no window adds a point equal to the running total.
  • A zero digit picks the all-zero point, which the routine does not handle, so its result is thrown away by a constant-time select.
  • It is also faster; see the rind-host-speed deck.

What broke

  • The translator lifts one point, sum0, out of the loop. The new routine overwrites it, so the loop's proof had to take that point as an input.
  • loop_spec and jac_base_multiply_spec in P256Base.lean were rewritten, with helpers ported from P384Keygen.lean.
