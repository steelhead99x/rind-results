# [L] P-384 ECDH and ECDSA are proven on x86_64 and aarch64

## Goal

P-384 key agreement and ECDSA earn the ✓ on x86_64 and aarch64, from the agreement and signature APIs to s2n-bignum's routines, as P-256's do on aarch64.

## Plan

- Done in [moq-dev/rind#628](https://github.com/moq-dev/rind/pull/628): P-384's group law over a curve-generic NIST layer (`Rind/Nist.lean`, `Rind/NistGroup.lean`), Pratt certificates for p and n with G's order (`Rind/P384Group.lean`, `Rind/Pratt.lean`), s2n-bignum's contracts for the field routines and `p384_montjscalarmul` on both targets, and `p384_agree` in `Rind/X86_64/P384.lean`, which `admin/prove` copies to aarch64: agreement with a held key, from decoding through d·Q and the conversion to affine.
- ✅ [moq-dev/rind#628](https://github.com/moq-dev/rind/pull/628) lands as a partial step (rejected: keep it open until the comb is proven), settled by the maintainer on 2026-11-11: the agreement proof stands on its own, the Rust comb proof is a new kind of proof, and both together are past one PR's review size.
- Remaining runs: rind's Rust comb for k·G (`base_multiply`: 20 tables of 16 affine points over `Scalar::booth_w5`'s 77 Booth-5 windows, `mid/p384/precomp.rs`), the affine table select (AVX2 or SSE2 intrinsics on x86_64, NEON on aarch64), verification's single-chain wNAF double multiplication (`public_twin_multiply`), encoding, and the key generation, loading, signing, and verification glue. #628 reshaped all of it for Aeneas, and `admin/prove` translates it on both targets.
- k·G first: it gives `p384_generate`, `p384_from_scalar`, and `p384_public_key`, which complete ECDH, and signing needs it too. Its proof is new: P-256's k·G runs s2n-bignum's `p256_scalarmulbase`, so no rind proof covers a Rust comb yet. It needs the Booth recoding's sum, a table proof like `Rind/Aarch64/P256Table.lean` that table j holds 1 to 16 times 2^(20j)·G, and that the comb's mixed additions never meet the equal points `p384_montjmixadd` excludes.
- The select intrinsics: model them as `Rind/X86_64/Simd.lean` does, or replace them with s2n-bignum's proven `bignum_copy_row_from_table` if that measures as fast. Modeling looks likelier: the generic routine reads every word of the 16-row table one at a time, and its NEON `_8n` form wants rows a multiple of 8 words, not P-384's 12.
- ECDSA: reuse P-256's structure (`P256Multiples.lean`, `P256Ecdsa.lean`, `P256Signature.lean`) over `Rind/Nist.lean`'s `ecdsaSign` and `ecdsaVerifies`, which already take the curve's p, n, and G. Verification settles equal and opposite points by cases before s2n-bignum's additions, whose proofs exclude them; prove those cases as P-256's proof does. Its contracts for `p384_montjdouble`, `p384_montjmixadd`, `p384_montjadd` (x86_64 only), `bignum_neg_p384`, `bignum_mod_n384`, and the generic Montgomery routines go into `Rind/<Arch>/S2nBignum.lean` as the proofs reach them.
- A parsed public key's order needs [the curve proofs' group assumptions](/quest/m4/verified/curve-groups.md), or takes the same `Order` hypothesis P-256's does until then.
- Once k·G is proven, `benchmarks/verified.toml` gains P-384 ECDH's entries, and the ECDSA ones with the signature proofs.
