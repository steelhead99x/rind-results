# [L] P-256 ECDH and ECDSA are proven on x86_64

## Goal

P-256 key agreement, ECDSA signing, and ECDSA verification, which every TLS certificate chain with an ECDSA key exercises, earn the ✓ on x86_64, as they do on aarch64.

## Plan

- Runs: for k·G, aws-lc's `ecp_nistz256_select_w7` (SSE2) or `ecp_nistz256_avx2_select_w7`, and `ecp_nistz256_point_add_affine` or its BMI2 and ADX form `_affinex` with their field helpers, under rind's Booth-7 driver over a 152 KB table of 37 rows of 64 affine points (`mid/p256.rs`, `low/x86_64/ecp_nistz256.rs`); for d·Q, s2n-bignum's proven `p256_montjscalarmul` and rind's conversion to affine; and the ECDSA and agreement glue that `proofs/Rind/Aarch64/` already proves for aarch64, where the code is shared.
- Already proven: every s2n-bignum routine; on aarch64, all the shared Rust. s2n-bignum's proven x86_64 `p256_scalarmulbase` cost 12-17% ([moq-dev/rind#234](https://github.com/moq-dev/rind/pull/234)), so it is no route.
- Route:
  1. d·Q: measure s2n-bignum's proven `p256_scalarmul`, which returns affine coordinates as aarch64's does, against today's. Switch if it is as fast; otherwise prove the conversion to affine with Aeneas.
  2. k·G: HOL Light proofs of aws-lc's select and mixed-addition routines in s2n-bignum's x86_64 model against its P-256 specs, then Aeneas for the Booth driver, and a proof that the x86_64 table holds the multiples of G those proofs assume, as `Rind/Aarch64/P256Table.lean` does. Verification's u1·G reads the same table.
  3. Run `admin/prove`'s P-256 proofs on x86_64, with its own axioms in `Rind/X86_64/S2nBignum.lean` (the ADX routines and their `_alt` forms).
- Upstream: offering this work to awslabs/s2n-bignum is optional, and there is no standing approval: stop and ask the maintainer before posting there or anywhere outside the moq-dev and kixelated orgs (decided with the maintainer on 2026-11-07 on [moq-dev/rind#448](https://github.com/moq-dev/rind/pull/448)).
