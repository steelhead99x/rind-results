# [XL] armv7's Curve25519 is proven

## Goal

X25519 and Ed25519 earn the ✓ on armv7.

## Plan

- Runs: curve25519-dalek's serial 32-bit backend for X25519 key generation's base multiplication and all of Ed25519, under rind's canonical-y and small-order checks; with NEON, BoringSSL's `neon2` X25519 assembly (from SUPERCOP) for the shared secret. HACL*'s X25519 was 1.9x slower than `neon2` and its Ed25519 3.5x slower to sign, so switching fails the speed rule.
- Already proven: nothing rind's `proofs/` imports. Beneficial AI Foundation's Lean proofs of curve25519-dalek, through Aeneas, moved on 2026-11-05 from `curve25519-dalek-lean-verify`, whose [README](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/curve25519-dalek-lean-verify/blob/52cad87d054943c96c984d99d9ea9e5d043b0d65/README.md) now points away, to [a fork of curve25519-dalek](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/curve25519-dalek-fork/tree/95b5831c63ff1c5f6287bd3a77569412a9afdbfb/curve25519-dalek/lean/Proofs). The old repository covered the serial 64-bit backend only; the fork also holds [`Backend/Serial/U32`](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/curve25519-dalek-fork/tree/95b5831c63ff1c5f6287bd3a77569412a9afdbfb/curve25519-dalek/lean/Proofs/Backend/Serial/U32), the 32-bit field, scalar, and constants, added between 2026-10-26 and 2026-11-02. Its `Field/Mul`, `Field/SquareInner`, `Field/ToBytes`, and `Scalar/MontgomeryReduce` had no `sorry` on 2026-11-09; the rest is unchecked.
- Route: start from the fork's 32-bit proofs, checking first that every file is complete (the fork's crate is 5.0.0, the version in rind's `Cargo.lock`), and write with Aeneas only what they lack. Reuse their point and scalar layers and specs where they are backend-generic, and prove rind's glue against `Rind/Ed25519.lean`'s spec; `neon2` on [the AArch32 model](/quest/m5/verified-armv7/model.md).

## Required

- [armv7 runs the Rust proofs](/quest/m5/verified-armv7/prove.md) - the armv7 translation
- [An AArch32 instruction model checks armv7's assembly](/quest/m5/verified-armv7/model.md) - the model `neon2`'s proof runs on
